Who We Are

About Us

Education with values, discipline and care

Welcome to Our School

Kripaon Ki Mata High School, located in Raja Ka Tajpur, Bijnor, is dedicated to providing quality education to children of our village and nearby areas. We believe every child has the potential to shine when given the right guidance and environment.

Our experienced teachers focus on strong fundamentals, moral values, and modern skills so that students are ready for higher studies and for life.
